How EduConnect Works in 3 Simple Steps

A simple, powerful digitalized tuition platform connecting neighborhood students & teachers

1

1. Take Student & Teacher Data

Students and teachers enter their academic details (Class 6–12, CBSE/ICSE/State, learning subjects) and pin their GPS coordinates via the browser's live location API.

Data Profiles + GPS Coordinates
2

2. Match with Haversine Algorithm

The backend applies spherical trigonometry with Earth's radius (R = 6,371 km) to calculate the exact physical distance in kilometers, filtering and ranking nearby educators.

Haversine Distance Engine (km)
3

3. Create Cohorts & Live Chat

Teachers or students create localized study batches (Cohorts). Members share syllabus notes, formula PDFs, and collaborate in real-time with live chat polling.
